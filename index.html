I am a Ph.D. student in Computer Science and Technology at Shanghai Jiao Tong University, advised by Prof. Yuntian Chen and Prof. Cewu Lu.

My research lies at the intersection of AI Safety, AI4Science, and automated scientific discovery. I am interested in the safety, alignment, and behavior of large language models, as well as LLM-based knowledge discovery, equation discovery, and interpretable scientific machine learning.
